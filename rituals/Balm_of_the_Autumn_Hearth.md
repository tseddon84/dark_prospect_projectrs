# Balm of the Autumn Hearth

**Requires:** Changeling, (Primal Art OR Satyr Kith), Rituals 2
**Components:** A physical prop to act as the Talen (e.g., a woven charm, a polished stone, a vial of pure water), **OR** up to three fully crafted Herbalism Healing Potions, **OR** up to three distinct servings of Alcohol (Satyr Ritualists only).
**Level:** 2
**Cost:** 3 Glamour
**Cast Time:** 10 minutes
**Notes:** Requires an ST to issue the resulting item tags. 
**Purpose:** Create a consumable magic item (Talen) that removes the Aggravated Damage condition.

## Description
This ritual channels the restorative energies of the Dreaming to soothe wounds that violently reject normal healing. By weaving Glamour into a physical object, the ritualist creates a one-use consumable item capable of curing severe supernatural injuries. 

*   **Standard Method:** The ritualist infuses a mundane prop with Glamour, transforming it into a single Talen. When crushed, consumed, or activated by a player, the user may touch a target (or themselves) and call "Remove Agg" to strip the Aggravated Damage condition. This method yields exactly **one (1)** Talen.
*   **Herbalism Infusion:** If the ritualist utilizes fully crafted Healing Potions (from the Mortal Herbalism skill) as the base components, the Glamour synergizes with the existing medicinal properties. Instead of producing 1 Talen, the 3 Glamour cost infuses up to **three (3)** Healing Potions. When consumed, these infused potions heal their normal health levels *and* allow the user to call "Remove Agg". 
*   **Satyr's Brew:** A Satyr ritualist may bypass the need for the Primal Art by utilizing their Kith's inherent connection to revelry and endurance. By using Alcohol as the component instead of potions or props, the 3 Glamour cost infuses up to **three (3)** distinct servings of Alcohol. When consumed, the alcohol functions as normal (providing its usual intoxicating effects), but also allows the drinker to call "Remove Agg". 

All items created by this ritual expire at the end of the game weekend if unused.

## Performance
The ritualist creates a comforting space (using blankets, a hearth fire, or soothing incense). They place the target component(s) in the center. Over the course of 10 minutes, the ritualist must sing a lullaby, recount a tale of profound healing, or (in the case of the Satyr's Brew) lead a boisterous toast to health and survival. Once the time has elapsed, the Glamour is spent. The ritualist must visit ST camp to procure item tags for the Talens or update their Potion/Alcohol tags with the "Remove Agg" property.

---
*Designer Notes: As the core rules shift to make Aggravated Damage harder to remove, this ritual creates a dedicated mechanical bypass that makes Changelings highly sought-after healers. By explicitly allowing Herbalism potions and Satyr alcohol to act as a multiplier, we create a strong economic bridge between Mortal crafting skills and Changeling magic, encouraging cross-splat trading without breaking the economy.*
