# Tale of the True Name

**Requires:** Changeling, (Naming OR Eshu Kith), Rituals 4
**Components:** The True Name of the object or person; the target itself inside the ritual circle (or a closely tied sympathetic object, such as a prized possession or lock of hair).
**Level:** 4
**Cost:** 3 Glamour
**Cast Time:** 10 minutes
**Notes:** Requires ST participation. The ritualist must give STs ample time to prepare the story before casting this ritual, or it will fail.
**Purpose:** Learn the subjective, un-manipulated story of a specific event from the perspective of a person or object.

## Description
This ritual uncovers the unwritten story etched into the very pattern of a person or object. Because it reads the True Name's connection to the Dreaming rather than the physical mind, it bypasses mental manipulations, supernatural memory wipes, or mundane forgetfulness. However, it is not an objective recording; the story is bound entirely to the target's perspective. 

*   **For a Person:** The narrative reflects exactly what they perceived and felt at the time, but is strictly limited by their physical and supernatural senses. If they were blindfolded, the story only includes sounds and physical sensations. If they lack the ability to perceive the supernatural, the story will not include supernatural details. Furthermore, the story recounts *what* happened and *what they felt*, not *why* they did it or their internal logic. They might recount seeing an upside-down symbol, but the story won't explain that they knew it was a warning.
*   **For an Object:** The narrative is highly reliable but extremely narrow. A weapon remembers the clash of steel, the strength of the wielder, and the spray of blood, but it has no concept of what the bystanders were doing or what words were spoken.

## Performance
The ritualist draws a circle and places the target (or sympathetic tie) within it. Over the course of 10 minutes, the ritualist must recite an invocation demanding the target's story, explicitly using its True Name multiple times. 

The ritualist must define a specific, singular event they wish to learn about (e.g., "the night Steve the vagrant was murdered"). The ritual cannot answer aggregate or broad questions like "how many times have you killed people?" 

Once completed, an ST (or designated NPC with an orange headband) will act as the "Story." They will approach the ritualist and whisper the events of the requested incident. The ST determines the exact perspective and limitations of the information provided based on the target's nature and sensory capabilities.

---
*Designer Notes: Changelings deal in stories. Reading the unwritten "story" recorded on a True Name rather than reading a physical, biological brain perfectly fits the Glamour and Dreaming paradigm. Because it bypasses memory-altering powers like Dominate or Chicanery, it has a heavy system impact. However, it is balanced by the extreme difficulty of acquiring a target's True Name. We limit cognitive load by having an ST narrate the vision directly, preventing players from having to ad-lib complex metaphysical rules on the fly.*
