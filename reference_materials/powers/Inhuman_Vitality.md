# Inhuman Vitality

When targeted by a status effect, disease effect, Ongoing effect, or the effects of a Tagged Poison, spend the listed energy and call “Resist” to ignore the triggering effect. Each time you use this power before Recovery, the cost increases by 1 to a maximum of 3. When you Recover, the cost resets back to its base level.<noinclude>[[Category:Powers]]</noinclude>
