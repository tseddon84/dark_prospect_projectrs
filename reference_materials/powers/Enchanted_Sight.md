# Enchanted Sight

''Buff, Sight.'' This power allows you to see, but not hear, characters on the other side of the gauntlet from you, regardless of which side you are on. You are aware which side the gauntlet someone is on, or if they're in both. You may not target across the gauntlet (including sensory powers) without Breach or similar.<noinclude>[[Category:Powers]]</noinclude>
