# Sense Deeply

''Mental, Instant, Sensory''. Use the sig call "Deep Sense" - you may ask the target three of the following questions: their current Willpower rating, if they are under a Mental Effect, how many ritual effects they are under, if they have Magic Susceptibility, If they have the Infernalist effect on them, or if they are under a Blood Bond, Charm, Entrancement, or Obsession. Shifters, Gaian Spirits, and Kinfolk may ask if the target is Kinfolk. Vampires and Ghouls may ask the target's Path, Path rating, if they have committed Diablerie, or if they are a Ghoul. Changelings and Kinain may ask the target's Court, Kith, and if the are Kinain. You may use this power multiple times on a single target to ask 3 questions for each expenditure. Note that unlike most Sensory powers, you may not waive the Resource cost of Sense Deeply by studying the target for 10 seconds.<noinclude>[[Category:Powers]]</noinclude>
