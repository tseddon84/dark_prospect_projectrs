# Sense Bond

''Mental, Instant, Sensory''. Use the sig call "Sense Bond, are you under the effects of a bond or bond-like effect?” The target must answer honestly. You may choose to ignore the power's resource cost by spending a silent 10-count obviously observing the target while standing within conversational distance of them. If the target leaves conversational range during this silent 10 count, or you move, use another power or attack someone, the count is interrupted and must be restarted.
