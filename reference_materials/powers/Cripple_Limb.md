# Cripple Limb

''Status, Physical.'' Spend the required energy and call “Cripple [Limb]” and throw a packet at the target. If the target is hit by the packet, they lose the ability to use the called limb. Valid limbs for this power are the Right Arm, Left Arm, Right Leg or Left Leg. If the limb was an arm, they must drop whatever they were holding in that hand (or place it on the ground if it is fragile) and cannot pick up, use items or target powers using that arm. If they were holding a two-handed weapon or other item in both hands they may retain a grip on it with their other hand (though they cannot use any item which requires two hands to operate with only one functional arm). If the limb was a leg the target must limp or drag that leg and cannot move faster than a slow hobble while the leg remains Crippled.<noinclude>[[Category:Powers]]</noinclude>
