# Sense Liability

''Mental, Instant, Sensory''. Use the sig call: "Sense Liability: Are you under any negative effects?" - the target must answer yes if they are under any harmful Mental, Status, or Physical effect. You may not choose to use a more precise call. You may choose to ignore the power's resource cost by spending a silent 10-count obviously observing the target while standing within conversational distance of them. If the target leaves conversational range during this silent 10 count, or you move, use another power or attack someone, the count is interrupted and must be restarted.<noinclude>[[Category:Powers]]</noinclude>
