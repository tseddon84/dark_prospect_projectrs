# Ritual:Purity of Flesh

{{Ritual
|Thaumaturgy|Vampire|Ghoul|
|name = Purity of Flesh
|level = 1
|requires = Thaumaturgy, Rituals 1
|cost = 2 blood
|components = 13 candles
|target = Self or other
|casttime = 2 minutes
|description = Create a level 1 Thaumaturgy Circle. 
Place the 13 candles around the circle at regular distances. Target must sit or kneel in the center of the circle.

Recite the following passage as you light each candle:

"This candle represents the purity of the blood of my forebears."

When all the candles are lit, place your hands on the target and silently meditate for 60 seconds. Recite the following:

"I am a master of my own will,<br>
I am a master of my own blood.<br>
I will my blood to be pure,<br>
As pure as those before the Flood."

Spend 2 Blood.

Immediately end any mental effect, as well as any disease, poison, illness, drug or alcohol effect currently on the target. All drug counters are reset to 0.

The ritual is complete. Roleplay that the target's hands are covered in ash until washed.
