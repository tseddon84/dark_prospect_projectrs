# Ritual:Recure the Homeland

{{Ritual
|Thaumaturgy|Vampire|Ghoul|
|name = Recure of the Homeland
|level = 2
|requires = Thaumaturgy, Rituals 2
|cost = 3 blood
|components = A container of loose soil, a method to mark the skin.
|casttime = 3 minutes
|description = 
'''Target''': Either self, or another conscious character with at least 1 health level.

Create a level 2 Thaumaturgy Circle. 

Place your target in the center of the circle. Arrange the components around the circle, with one on each point.

Meditate on each of the components for 1 minute each.

Have the target work the soil with their hands. Then, draw a sigil of healing on the backs of both of the target’s hands and their forehead.

Caster spends 3 blood.

The target should roleplay being very sleepy, and within a few moments succumb to Irresistible Merciful Sleep, 10 minutes, cannot be awoken. As the target sleeps, the sigils will fade. When they awake, they will be completely healed, any aggravated damage will be removed, and any mental effects will be removed.
}}
