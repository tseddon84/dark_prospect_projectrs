|description = Create a level 3 Thaumaturgy Circle.
 
Sit in the center of the circle. Arrange the components around the circle, with one on each point.

On the parchment, draw a large sigil you feel is repellent to shifters. It should cover most of the parchment. 

At the bottom of the parchment write the following: “OOG Ward vs Lupines. If this parchment is removed from the door, remove the corresponding 3x5 on the outside of the door.”

After the drawing is complete, repeat the following 3 times:
Go over the same lines with the Silver Focus, chanting the following:
“By my will, this passage is proof from the changing breeds.”
Silently meditate on the parchment for 60 seconds

Spend 5 blood. Destroy the tag on the Silver focus.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
 This Door is ritually Warded vs those who use Gnosis.
 Ritual: Ward vs. Lupines
 Caster: (Your name)
 Begin Time: (Current time and date)
 Duration: 4 hours
 Effect: This door is Warded against anyone who may spend Energy as Gnosis, and they may not pass through this door. This ward will end if the Parchment on the other side of the door is removed.

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual, and it should not be made in advance.
Affix the parchment to the inside of the door, and the 3x5 to the opposite side.
The ritual will end if the parchment is removed from the inside of the door by anyone.

Any given door can only have one Ward Vs ritual cast on it at a time.
