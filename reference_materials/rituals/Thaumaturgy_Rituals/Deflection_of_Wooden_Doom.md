# Ritual:Deflection of Wooden Doom

{{Ritual
|Thaumaturgy|Vampire|Ghoul|
|name = Deflection of the Wooden Doom
|level = 1
|requires = Thaumaturgy, Rituals 1

|cost = 1 blood
|components = A length of wood, at least 1 inch in length (a toothpick will do.) A candle.

|target = 
|casttime = 3 minutes
|description = Create a level 1 Thaumaturgy Circle. 
Hold the wood in one hand and the candle in the other. Silently medidate for 60 seconds.
Pantomime blackening the tips of the wood in the flame of the candle.

Recite the following passage three times:

"I put this wood<br>
to the flame,<br>
and doing so,<br>
I here proclaim;

Should another<br>
wish to clash,<br>
turn their stake<br>
into ash."

Spend 1 Blood.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
 Ritual: Deflection of the Wooden Doom
 Caster: (Your name)
 Begin Time: (Current time and date)
 Duration: 24 hours
 Effect: The first time anyone attempts to use a staking mechanic on you, you must call resist. Then, call {{PowerTip|Shatter}} against the object that attempted to stake you. The Shatter is resistible as normal.

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual, and it should not be made in advance.
Carry the 3x5 with you while the ritual is in effect.
This ritual will end immediately if you do not carry the wooden focus on your person.
