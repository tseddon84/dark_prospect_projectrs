# Ritual:Mirror of Second Sight

{{Ritual
|Thaumaturgy|Vampire|Ghoul|
|name = Mirror of Second Sight
|level = 3
|requires = Thaumaturgy, Rituals 3
|cost = 3 blood
|components = A hand mirror, a bladed weapon, a symbol of the supernatural
|target = 
|casttime = 3 minutes
|description = The symbol can be anything that represents a non-vampire supernatural to your character.

Create a level 3 Thaumaturgy Circle. 

Sit in the center of the circle. Arrange the components around the circle, with one on each point.

Repeat the following actions 3 times:
Take the weapon in hand, pantomime cutting yourself and bleeding onto the mirror. 
Replace the weapon on its point. Pick up the mirror and pass it over the symbol, while reciting the following:

"Magical beast, I spy you in the mirror,<br>
Your mask falls away, I see your reflection clearer."

Replace the mirror to its location and silently meditate for 60 seconds.

Spend 3 blood.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
 Ritual: Mirror of Second Sight
 Caster: (Your name)
 Begin Time: (Current time and date)
 Duration: 24 hours
 Effect: While holding the mirror, you can look at the reflection of another person and use the following powers:
 Sense Supernatural, No Cost, Other
 Sense Supernatural: Shifter, 1 Blood, Other
 Sense Supernatural: Vampire, 1 Blood, Other
 Sense Supernatural: Wraith, 1 Blood, Other
 Sense Supernatural: Spirit, 1 Blood, Other

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual, and it should not be made in advance.
Affix the card to the back of the mirror.

}}
