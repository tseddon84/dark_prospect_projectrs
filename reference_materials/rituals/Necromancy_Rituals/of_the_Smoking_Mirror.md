# Ritual:Ritual of the Smoking Mirror

{{Ritual
|Vampire|Necromancy|Ghoul|
|name = Ritual of the Smoking Mirror
|level = 1
|requires = Necromancy, Rituals 1
|cost =  1 blood
|components = A hand mirror.
|purpose = 
|casttime = 3 minutes
|notes = 
|description = Prepare your Necromantic Focus. 

Repeat the following 5 times:

Study your own reflection in the mirror for 60 seconds. Then say the following chant:

"Mirror, Mirror, <br>
hear my plea;<br>
let your reflection,<br>
allow me to see;<br>
how hurt they are, <br>
how shallow breath;<br>
how very close,<br>
they are to death."

Spend 1 blood.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
<pre class="card">
Ritual: Ritual of the Smoking Mirror
Caster: (Your name)
Begin Time: (Current time and date)
Duration: 1 hour.
Effect: For the duration, the caster gains the following abilities against targets the caster can see in the mirror:
Assess, as per medicine 1, No Cost, Other
Aura Sense, No Cost, Other, 3 questions
</pre>

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual; it should either be done at the time of casting, or the caster must silently meditate for 1 minute if a preprinted card is used.

Affix the 3x5 card to the back of the mirror.
}}
