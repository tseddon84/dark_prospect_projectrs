# Ritual:Bastone Diabolico

{{Ritual
|Vampire|Necromancy|Ghoul|
|name = Bastone Diabolico
|level = 4
|requires = Necromancy, Rituals 4, Melee
|cost = 1 blood
|components = A white boffer, the size of a human thigh bone. A large wooden basin/ bowl

|casttime = 10 minutes
|notes = A necromancer can only cast this ritual once per day.
|description = Prepare your necromantic foci

Pantomime draining your blood into the wooden basin, and then ritually bathing the bone.

Spend 1 blood.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
<pre class="card">
Ritual: Bastone Diabolico
Caster: (Your name)
Begin Time: (Current time and date)
Duration: Until Dawn.
Effect: Only the caster may use this weapon. This weapon strikes with the following power: Energy Burn 1, 1 Blood, Cross-Gauntlet only. This weapon cannot be used for any other damage call, nor can anything increase the damage.
</pre>

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual; it should either be done at the time of casting, or the caster must silently meditate for 1 minute if a preprinted card is used.

Affix the card to the bone.
