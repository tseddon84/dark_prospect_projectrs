|description = Prepare your necromantic foci

Using the salt or chalk, make a circle you can stand in, no larger in diameter than about 10 feet.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
<pre class="card">
Ritual: Circle of Cerberus
Caster: (Your name)
Begin Time: (Current time and date)
Duration: Until Dawn, or until the Caster leaves the circle.
Effect: Those in the circle are unable to affect, or be affected by those things across the gauntlet. Additionally, crossing the gauntlet is impossible inside the circle. Finally, the circle prevents anyone who can spend energy as Pathos from entering the circle once it has been cast. While inside the circle, call “Immune” to any cross-gauntlet action.
</pre>

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual; it should either be done at the time of casting, or the caster must silently meditate for 1 minute if a preprinted card is used.

Put the card on the circle of salt or chalk.

If you leave the circle, the ritual ends.
