# Ritual:Rite of Talisman Dedication

|purpose=To allow mundane items to be usable in the Umbra. This ritual can be used with pieces of ammunition, however you must spend 1 Gnosis per dart and the dart is no longer usable in the Umbra once it has been fired once. Primarily used for melee weapons but may be used on any mundane item, tagged or not.
|description=
Performing this ritual removes any current Dedications and only dedicates the items you choose during the newly performed ritual.

Variations on this ritual are allowed, (you may add steps, and we highly recommend doing so!) however the core of the ritual is described below and must remain in-tact.

Place all items you wish to dedicate on the ground in front of yourself. Name each item with it's own unique name, then describe it's purpose. Pick each item up and place it on your person in some way. For foam darts immediately load them into a gun and keep that gun on you. Recite: "I am [character name], [rank] of the [Tribe/Aspect/Fera-type]. These tools will aid me in my duty." Spend an amount of Gnosis equal to the number of items you are dedicating. For the next 24 hours these items become Relics-- tie a small piece of white yarn or thread to each affected prop to indicate that it is a temporary Relic.

If used on one or more pieces of ammunition, the targeted darts do not become Relics. Instead, the user of the rite may fire a number of foam darts equal to the number of darts affected while in the Umbra, adding "Breach" to the damage call in order to inform the target that the bullet in question hit them in the Umbra. The ritualist does not need to keep track of which exact darts they've affected, simply the total number of darts they are entitled to use.
