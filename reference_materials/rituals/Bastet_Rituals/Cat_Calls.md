# Ritual:Cat Calls

{{Ritual
|Bastet|Fera|
|name=Cat Calls
|requires=1 Bastet
|level=1
|cost=1 Gnosis
|renown=Cunning
|casttime=5 minutes
|purpose=Allows the caster to create a special "Bastet-only" communication card.
|description=
Spend about 5 minutes silently exploring the ‘town’ site - ignore any who may address you. Anything other than silent inspection of the site will break the Rite. Fill out one side of a 3x5 card as follows:

<pre>
Ritual: Cat Calls
The text on this card is out of game to any non-Bastet. It cannot be read by others, even if your powers would normally allow you to do so. This card may only be removed by its creator. 

[message to other Bastet]
</pre>

On the other side of the 3x5 card, write in large text:

<pre>
ALL BASTET MAY READ THIS
NO OTHERS MAY
</pre>

Spend 1 Gnosis, then stick the card to a prominent location (tape or tack), ALL BASTET side up. Please note, you do not need to include your name or your character’s name on this card
}}
