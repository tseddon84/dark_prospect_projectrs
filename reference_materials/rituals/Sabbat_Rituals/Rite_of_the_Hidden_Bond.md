# Ritual:Rite of the Hidden Bond

|purpose = Initially developed by the isolationist Gangrel Muricia, based on observed Garou rituals, this modified version of The Vaulderie serves to bridge between the unnatural undead state of vampirism and the vibrant spirit world, allowing a Spirit to become a Totem of a Sabbat Pack. Originally held in some secrecy by Muricia and her brood, the Rite has leaked out into the wider Sabbat in the New World, where it is very occasionally practiced by Kolduns or particularly spiritual Nomadic packs. It is exceptionally rare, however, and the number of Cainites who know the rite outside of Muricia’s own childer can be counted on one's hands– perhaps, however, you are one of them.
|description = 

This rite is performed precisely as the Vaulderie is, with the following changes:
:: *A Gaian spirit willing to become the Pack’s totem must be present, uncoerced and conscious.
:: *After the initial invocation (“My blood springs from Caine, and is gladly spilled in His service.”) the Ritemaster must add the line “My blood is the life of the world, and to the world it will return.” Upon speaking these words, the prospective Totem Appears to all participants (as the Power) and gains the ability to interact with the ritual chalice as though it were a Relic.
:: *After the final vampire fills the Chalice, it must be offered to the prospective Totem, who will breathe into it, spending a point of gnosis (and adding their bead to indicate they have done so). The Spirit should then hand the calice to the ritemaster, who continues as normal.
:: *Once the last vampire has drunk from the Calice, they should offer it to the Spirit, who should also drink (removing a bead as normal).

The Rite fails as normal for the Vaulderie (that is, if any beads are left over at the end, or not enough are in the Calice for everyone to take one). If it is successful, the Pack should welcome their new, mildly insubstantial member into the Night.

The Totem does not inflict or gain a Blood Bond on the other participants, however, as their essence lacks that ability– but the spiritual link of the Totem bond serves as a metaphysically equivalent.

The Ritemaster should also fill out a card with the following information and either give it to staff at game down, or write the information in the rituals tab of their Check Out form after game down:

Vaulderie Totem Bond<br>
Ritualist: [your character] ([your name])<br>
Pack: [name of your pack]<br>
Members: [each member of your pack]<br>
Totem: [the name of the spirit]

At the following check-in all participants should change the Patron on their character sheet to the name of the new Totem. They may now purchase the Totem background. Totems should expect to be treated as respected advisers and peers to the pack, not as superiors. Spirits with Bans that require subservience or personal obedience from their Pack members may not pack bond using the Vaulderie.
This ritual can be recast to add people to an existing pack, and/or Totem Bond.
