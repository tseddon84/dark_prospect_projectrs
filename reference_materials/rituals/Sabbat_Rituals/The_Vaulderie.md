# Ritual:The Vaulderie

{{Ritual
|Sabbat|Vampire|
|name = The Vaulderie
|level = 1
|requires = Ritemaster of the Sabbat, Rituals 1
|cost = 1 blood point from each participant (which is refunded upon completion of the Rite), 1 willpower from the Ritemaster
|components = A cup or other drinking vessel, 3-13 vampires willing to become a Pack, a number of beads, pebbles or other small objects equal to the number of participants
|casttime = 5 minutes
|purpose = To break the chains of the hated Blood Bond and reaffirm the bonds of community and struggle that ties the Sabbat together
|description = To begin this ritual, the Ritemaster should distribute 1 bead, small stone or similar sized object to each participant out of game, and then gather all of the participants into a circle and stand in the center, with a chalice and a consecrated knife. The Ritemaster will then give a short sermon of Caine or inspirational speech. The Ritemaster then chants the ceremonial words of the Vaulderie:

“My blood springs from Caine, and is gladly spilled in His service.”

Thereafter, he pantomimes cutting her wrist and bleeding into the cup. The Ritemaster then carries the chalice around the circle, so that each vampire in the ritual can speak the same blessing and add her blood to the mix. Traditionally, the officiant takes chalice to the most prestigious vampire participating in the ritual and then proceeds to the left from that point. Each participant who adds her blood to the Chalice (including the Ritemaster) should also place her bead or token into it, to indicate her contribution. It is exceptionally difficult to pretend to contribute without it being obvious to the Ritemaster, but may be possible via clever use of powers or collusion between the ritemaster and shirker.

Each participant must spend 1 Blood.

Once everyone in the circle has contributed, the Ritemaster stands in the center of the circle once more and lifts the cup toward the heavens. She speaks the second ceremonial invocation of the Vaulderie, “Praise Caine! May the bonds be broken.”

The blood within the cup is transubstantiated and purified by the ritual. This act removes the taint of disease or drugs, and negates powers that leave lingering harmful effects in the blood (such as Acid Blood).

The Ritemaster then drinks from the chalice, thanking Caine for his blessing. He carries the chalice around the circle again, beginning with the most prestigious and then moving to the left of that individual. Each repeats the second invocation and drinks from the chalice, until the final vampire in the ritual drains the chalice dry. Each participant who genuinely drinks from the chalice should remove a bead from it to indicate they have done so. If a participant receives the chalice to drink and finds it empty of beads (indicating that at least one person did not fill it), they should report this to the Ritemaster and the Rite will immediately fail. Further, if the chalice returns to the ritemaster with one or more beads remaining in it (indicating someone has not drunk), the rite will also immediately fail.

If the ritual is successful, all participants receive the following:
Break Bonds: All blood bonds EXCEPT those created by the Vaulderie Ritual, and those from clan flaws.
Each member then gains (or increments by 1) a blood bond with every other participant in the ritual.
SPECIAL: Vaulderie cannot create a level 3 blood bond under any circumstances.
}}
