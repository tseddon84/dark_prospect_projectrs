# Ritual:Oath of Binding

{{Ritual
|Sabbat|Vampire|
|name = The Oath of Binding
|level = 2
|requires = Ritemaster of the Sabbat, Rituals 2
|cost = None
|components = At least 3 full members of the Sabbat, the Ritemaster
|casttime = 10-30 minutes
|purpose = Held each year on Winter Solstice, this rite constitutes a formal renewal of a vampire’s oath of allegiance to the Sabbat. All True Sabbat are expected to attend this holiday, reaffirming their dedication in a public rite of loyalty. As part of the ritual, the local clergy must hear all grievances brought before them. Grievances addressed during the night of Binding are traditionally forgiven, though vampires are petty creatures, more than capable of lying through their sharpened teeth.
|description = This ritual can only be performed during the game closest to the Winter Solstice. It must be performed at night, and requires at least 3 Sabbat members. It should be conducted by the highest ranked Clergy, but may be assisted by a lower clergy if the highest does not know the rite.
The Ritemaster leads the Sabbat in a renewal of the Oath given during the Creation Rites. 

“Of my own free will and accord, I, (name),<br>
son/daughter of Caine, reject the Antediluvians<br>
and dedicate myself to their destruction.<br>
I swear my soul to the Sabbat,<br>
and damn all those who stand against us.<br>
Hear me, brothers and sisters! Your enemies are my enemies.<br>
Your victories, my victories. Your blood, my blood.<br>
So shall it be, until the end of all things.”<br>
— The Oath of the Sabbat

If any participant refuses (or is prevented) from swearing the oath the Rite immediately fails. The offender is usually torn apart by frenzying vampires soon after.

The Ritemaster then spends 1 blood per participant in the rite. The Ritemaster is permitted to pause to feed to be able to spend enough energy for very large groups.

The Ritemaster then serves as a judge or councilor for any and all grievances brought up by any member of the Sabbat. Once a grievance has been aired and mediated, it is officially considered resolved and is not tolerated to be brought up again– though a Sabbat member unsatisfied with the resolution may call for a Monomacy against the offending party, which may be resolved only after the end of the Solstice night at the earliest.

Effect: For the remainder of the game that this ritual is performed all ritual participants are allowed a free resist to any Frenzy once per day.
}}
