# Ritual:Rite of the Fetish Egg

{{Ritual
|Corax|Fera|
|name=Rite of the Fetish Egg
|requires=1 Corax and 1 Corax Kinfolk or 1 mortal character of a willing Player
|components=1 egg prop, Rite of Talisman Dedication, an orange ribbon.
|level=2
|cost=3 PERMANENT Gnosis
|renown=Wisdom
|casttime=1-3 minutes
|purpose=Convert 1 mortal character or Corax Kinfolk into a full Corax.
|description=
Perform the Rite of Talisman Dedication on your egg prop. Enter the Umbra and locate your target, who should be asleep or resting in the realm. You should wake the character OOG if they are sleeping. Touch the egg prop to the target’s head, and whisper, “My life to your life, my soul to your soul. As I grew into something more, so shall you.” At this point you may move the egg away from the target’s head and wrap it completely in the orange ribbon. and spend 3 Gnosis.

Both you and the target should head to ST camp at your first available time, in order to a) decrease your maximum Gnosis by 3 and b) move the other player to their new character sheet. Return to game in an orange headband alongside the character, and follow them until you feel it is an appropriate time to send them through their first change. Call out “First Change: Frenzy.” and the player should immediately go into a Frenzy. Once you have done this, you may go to another location and return to game.
}}
