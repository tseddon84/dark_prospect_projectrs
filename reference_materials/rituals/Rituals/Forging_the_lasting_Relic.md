Passage: "Malleo , loquar ad te , ignis, et nunc et in perpetuum."<br />
This is what the economy of Stygia is based upon, and possibly its greatest malevolent practice.The economy of the soul trade, is that one item, is one soul. It matters not how big or small the item is, every one of them requires the forging of a soul.<br />
This ritual allows you to turn an Oboli into a Relic or even forge a Wraith or Plasmic character directly into an item.<br />
Place the Obolis, Wraith, or Plasmic by placing them next to a Soulforge, reciting the required phrase (the target does not suffer exposure damage from using this power). Place the target on the ground and spend 60 seconds roleplaying forging the target into the desired item then spend 2 Pathos. <br />
If you are directly forging a character, gain 4 Angst.<br />
If performed with less than 10 hours of game time remaining, these effects persist through the following game
