# Ritual:Illusion of Peaceful Death

{{Ritual
|Thaumaturgy|Vampire|Ghoul|
|name = Illusion of Peaceful Death
|level = 2
|requires = Thaumaturgy, Rituals 2
|cost = 1 blood
|components = A feather from a bird, a measure of blood from a mortal.
|casttime = 3 minutes
|description = 
Create a level 2 Thaumaturgy Circle. 

Sit or stand in the center of the circle. Arrange the components around the circle, with one on each point.

Meditate on each of the components for 1 minute each.

Pantomime immersing the feather in the blood.

Recite the following:

“No matter what may have<br>
caused this heart to cease,<br>
Let those who search<br>
find he died in peace.”<br>

Spend 1 blood.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
<pre class="card">Ritual: Illusion of Peaceful Death
Caster: (Your name)
Begin Time: (Current time and date)
Duration: Until end of game
Effect: The caster can use this feather once to conceal the nature of a death. By dusting a dying or unconscious body with this feather and repeating “It must have been his time to go. At least he went out in his sleep” the caster will mystically conceal the details of what happened. This grants the caster Vitalsigns, No cost, Other single unconscious or dying target. All answers to the questions must lead toward injury or death by natural causes.
The caster can program any number of answers, but may only affect a single target.</pre>

After a feather has been used in this way, the ritual ends. The same feather can be used again.
}}
