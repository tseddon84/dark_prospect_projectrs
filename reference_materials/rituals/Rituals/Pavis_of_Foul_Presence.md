# Ritual:Pavis of Foul Presence

{{Ritual
|Thaumaturgy|Vampire|Ghoul|
|name = Pavis of Foul Presence
|requires = Tremere, Thaumaturgy, Rituals 3
|level = 3
|components = A blue silken cord or ribbon. A symbol of love or passion. A boffer bladed melee weapon.
|cost = 3 blood
|casttime = 5 minutes
|notes =  May only be cast at night.
|description = Create a level 3 Thaumaturgy Circle. 

Sit or stand in the center of the circle. Arrange the components around the circle, with one on each point.

Meditate on each of the three components for 1 minute each.

Carefully tie the blue cord around your neck as a necklace. Place the symbol of love in your left hand and use the weapon in your right hand.

Recite the following:

"My passions are my own<br>
Despite what you might do,<br>
I rule my own heart<br>
I shall not be influenced by you."

Spend 3 blood.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
 Ritual: Pavis of Foul Presence
 Caster: (Your name)
 Begin Time: (Current time and date)
 Duration: 8 hours
 Effect: Enforced {{PowerTip|Counter}}:{{PowerTip|Charm}}, NC & Enforced {{PowerTip|Counter}}:{{PowerTip|Entrancement}}, NC. For the duration of the ritual, if anyone uses those powers on you, you must call "Resist, Counter [power]".

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual; it should either be done at the time of casting, or the caster must silently meditate for 1 minute if a preprinted card is used.
Carry the 3x5 with you while the ritual is in effect.

This ritual will end immediately if the blue ribbon is removed.
