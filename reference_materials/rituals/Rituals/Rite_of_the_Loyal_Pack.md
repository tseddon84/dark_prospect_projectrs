# Ritual:Rite of the Loyal Pack

{{Ritual
|Werewolf|
|name=Rite of the Loyal Pack
|requires=The Alpha of a Werewolf pack and at least 1 other pack member.
|level=3
|cost=2 Gnosis per pack member
|casttime=2+ minutes
|renown=Glory, Ferocity, Infamy
|purpose=Grants all pack members who participate in the ritual 1 Willpower, not to exceed their maximum. A character may not benefit from this rite more than once per hour, and any one user may not use it more than once an hour.
|description=
This rite may only be performed by a pack Alpha. It begins with the available packmates gathering together in a circle(ish). With permission, each player should place their hands on the shoulders of the character next to them. If the Totem is present, they should stand opposite the ritualist. All characters should look to the ritualist, who should speak: “[Brothers and/or Sisters], we stand at the ready as a pack.” Proceed to give a motivational speech, this speech should last no less than 2 minutes. Other members may participate in the speech (such as responding, or adding to what you say), but silence of more than 10 seconds at once breaks the Rite. End the speech and ask: “Do I have your faith and trust as Alpha of [pack name]?” All characters *must* reply for the ritual to continue. If any character does not answer in the affirmative, the ritual ends immediately. If all characters answer in the affirmative, the ritualist should continue “As we are a strong pack, we strengthen each other. Spend 2 Gnosis to gain 1 Willpower.” - each character present who spends 2 Gnosis gains 1 Willpower.
}}
