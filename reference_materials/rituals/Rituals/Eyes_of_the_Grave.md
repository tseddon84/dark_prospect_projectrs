# Ritual:Eyes of the Grave

{{Ritual
|Vampire|Necromancy|Ghoul|
|name = Eyes of the Grave
|level = 2
|requires = Necromancy, Rituals 2
|cost = 1 blood
|components = A portrait of your target, a pinch of grave soil.
|purpose = 
|casttime = 5 minutes
|notes = A caster may only have 1 Eyes of the Grave ritual active at any time.
|description = Prepare your Necromantic Foci.

Take the grave soil and smear it into the eyes of the portrait. 

Recite the following:

"Show my victim<br>
his very own grave<br>
so that his mind<br>
I can enslave"

Spend 1 blood.

On a white 3x5 card, fill out a Dreamshape for your target. The dream side should be an image of the target’s own death.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
<pre class="card">
Ritual: Eyes of the Grave
Caster: (Your name)
Target: (Target name)
Begin Time: (Current time and date)
Duration: Until Dawn.
Effect: The caster may destroy the portrait this card is attached to while within line of sight of the Target. Doing so allows the caster to use Daydream, No Cost, Target giving the Dreamshape card made during the ritual.
</pre>

Writing the 3x5 cards is part of casting the ritual, and it should not be made in advance.
Affix the 3x5 ritual card to the back of the portrait.
