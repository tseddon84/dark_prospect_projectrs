# Ritual:Rite of Monomacy

{{Ritual
|Sabbat|Vampire|
|name = Rite of Monomacy
|level = 3
|requires = Ritemaster of the Sabbat, Rituals 3
|cost = 1 blood per participant
|components = A bladed weapon prop, at least 2 duelists
|casttime = 5-15 minutes, or more depending on how long the duel lasts
|purpose = The ritual of Monomancy is used to settle disputes among True Sabbat. A priest cannot perform the Monomancy ritual for themselves.

There are many social aspects of the ritual that should be followed before the ritual itself is ever enacted: a formal Challenge, the selection of an Arbiter who should spend some time attempting to resolve the dispute without resort to a duel, and should assist in defining the terms of the duel if no other resolution is possible.

Monomancy is usually a social event as well as a political one. Those who are fighting in the duel, the Arbiter, and the Priest casting the rite are considered participants. Everyone else is considered a witness. 
|description = Once the Monomancy is ready to begin, the Priest gathers all present in the location where the Monomancy is to take place. The priest then begins an invocation to ask for Caine to witness these events and recites the details of the Challenge for all to hear. The details should include the names of the parties being challenged, the reason for the monomancy, and the terms determined for the fight.

The priest then walks the participants around the perimeter of the monomancy space - usually a 30’ circle. The Priest asks all witnesses to remove themselves from the interior of the space. Then the priest reminds everyone of the solemnity of the Monomacy and cautions no one to interfere.

The priest spends one blood per individual participating in the Monomacy. Pantomime injuring yourself with a blade and smearing a touch of blood on each participant.

If the ritual is successful, three effects occur. First, end all beneficial effects on everyone participating in the Monomancy. Second, Each participant gains a single use of Resist Any. This resist expires at the end of the ritual. Third, until the end of the Monomacy, all effects targeting any Participant originating from anyone who is not a Participant gain the Blatant rider. 

The fight then begins.

The Monomacy ritual ends when one of the conclusions defined in the terms occurs - usually a fight to torpor or driving the opponent out of the bounds of the circle. Fights to the final death are discouraged, but sadly common. Note that the Monomacy is a duel and must involve violence, defining terms such as "whoever wins a game of chess," while valid means of resolving a dispute between Cainites, are not legitimate uses of the Sacred Monomacy.
