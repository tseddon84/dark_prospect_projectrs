|description = Create a level 2 Thaumaturgy Circle. 

Sit in one half of the circle, with the candle and the vial of blood in the other half.

Perform the following three times:

Silently meditate on the vial for 60 seconds.

Pass the vial over the flame of the candle while reciting:

"Blackest Blood,<br>
darkest Night<br>
Bring your secrets,<br>
into light

Let the flame <br>
dispel the shroud,<br>
That cloak of mystery<br>
of which you're proud."

Spend 1 blood.

Equip an OOG headband and go find your target immediately. Show them this ritual. You may ask the following questions, to which your target must answer honestly, per Character Sheet.
* What is your name?
* Are you a Supernatural?
* Are you a Vampire?
* What Vampire Clan are you?
* What is your Generation?
* What is your Maximum Blood Pool?
* What is your Current Blood Pool?
* Are you currently under any Blood Bonds?
* If so, who are you Blood bound to?
* Do you have anyone Blood Bound to you?
* If so, who is Blood Bound to you?

Once you have asked your questions, return to your ritual circle immediately and remove your OOG headband. Destroy the blood tag.
If you are unable to find your target within 10 minutes, the ritual ends with no effect. Return to your ritual circle and remove your OOG headband.
