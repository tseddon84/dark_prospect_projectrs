# Ritual:Rite of Replenishment

{{Ritual

|name=Rite of Replenishment
|requires=1 Gurahl, Umbra Access
|components=1 Usable Gnosis Node
|level=2
|cost=No Cost
|casttime=2 minutes
|purpose=Once a day, the Gurahl may use this rite to completely replenish their Gnosis pool from a node where they may normally receive Gnosis regeneration.

This ritual may only be used once per day.

The Gurahl who wishes to perform this Rite should approach the Gnosis node while in the Umbra. Rub up against the object the node card is on from at least 2 different positions, and curl up at the base of the node. “Nap” for at least 1 minute, and awake at full Gnosis and full Rage.
