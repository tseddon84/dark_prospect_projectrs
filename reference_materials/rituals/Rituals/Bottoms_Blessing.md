# Ritual:Bottom's Blessing

The mask is given to the player so they can apply it to themselves. 

“Once you are restored, this will seem as if it was all just a vivid dream.”

Target will not know they have Visage active and will believe they are perfectly normal. This will last until they are hit with three instances of disbelief, or one hour. A veiled human who has this cast on them will think it was all just a vivid dream, no matter how illogical that may seem.
