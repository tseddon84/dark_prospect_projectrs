# Ritual:The Creation Rite

{{Ritual
|Sabbat|Vampire|
|name = The Creation Rite
|level = 1
|requires = Ritemaster of the Sabbat, Rituals 1
|cost = 1 point of fire damage, which the initiate must not resist or dodge.
|components = A Vampire who has proven themselves worthy of joining the Sabbat as a full member (called the Initiate), a Priest (Usually the Ritemaster), and the leader of a Pack who intends to accept the Initiate as a member. A metal weapon, a fire prop
|casttime = 5-15 minutes
|special = May be cast once per Initiate, ever.
|purpose = the Creation Rite is a formal and sacred ritual performed after a fledgling vampire has proven herself to the sect, has been educated, and is ready to be Initiated. As part of this ritual, the priest consecrates a flaming brand and presses it to a supplicant’s forehead while she recites her first oath of allegiance to the sect. All pack Priests are required to learn this rite.
|description = The Ritemaster gathers all of the participants and calls the group to order. They then introduce the initiate to the gathering, citing the initiate's full mortal name - and if the initiate wishes to change their name as a Kindred, their new name in full. The Priest tells the story of how the initiate has proven herself to be a member of the Sabbat; including any tales of derring-do.

The Priest then asks each member of the congregation to speak on behalf of the initiate. Once everyone has said their part, the Priest directly asks each participant (except the initiate) “Are you willing to accept <name> as your Brother/Sister in Blood?” If a simple majority of other participants answer in the negative, the ritual fails.

The Priest then spends 1 Willpower and all other participants spend 1 blood.

The Priest then asks the initiate if they are willing to truly join the Sabbat, swearing fealty to the Sect and to Caine. If the initiate declines, the ritual fails.

The Priest then produces a weapon that can inflict fire damage (for the purpose of this ritual, this can be done by holding a metal weapon in a lit fire prop for 30 seconds) and holds it to the initiate’s forehead whilst leading the initiate in an Oath of Fealty to the Sabbat. The burning weapon should successfully inflict at least 1 fire damage, else the ritual fails. 
Oaths differ per pack, but here is an example oath:
“Cast by the fires, I pledge allegiance.
As the Daughters and Sons, we pledge allegiance.
With full enthusiasm, I pledge allegiance.
In the Great Jyhad, we pledge allegiance.
To the Sword of Caine, I pledge allegiance.
I recognize the authority of the Archbishop, but to him I do not pledge allegiance;
To the Sword of Caine, I pledge allegiance.”

If successful, the initiate is considered a full member of the Sect - a True Sabbat. Additionally all members refill pool willpower. If the rite is unsuccessful, it may not be performed on the same initiate during the same game. This ritual is usually followed by a Vaulderie ritual.
