'''Special''': This ritual is only castable once per game session.

Create a level 5 Thaumaturgy Circle. 

Sit or stand in the center of the circle. Arrange the components around the circle, with one on each point.

Meditate on each of the components for 1 minute each.

Cover the stone with arcane symbols. Then pantomime washing the stone in the blood of at least 3 different types of creatures.

Light the candle. (Electric please, no real fire.)

Spend 1 blood.

Place the stone and the candle some place safe together, with the candle still lit.

Over the rest of the game session, you can at any time touch the stone and spend blood. Once you have spent a total of 10 blood, extinguish the candle and fill out a Ritual Card for “Stone of True Form.”

At next check-in, you will receive the following fetish:

<pre>Stone of True Form
Revert Form, 1 blood, Self/Other, Touch</pre>
