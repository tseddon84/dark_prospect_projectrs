|components = 5 or more ritual participants and the target being elevated to Clerical rank.
|casttime = 5 or more minutes
|purpose = Solemn and erotic, the Blood Bath is the rite to formally ordain a vampire, elevating her to a clergy position within the hierarchy of the sect. Typically, the vampire being ordained stands in a pool of blood, containing the lifeblood of several humans murdered for the rite, but this is not actually required. She is approached by each member of the Sabbat participating in the ritual. As that participant places some of her blood into the pool, she may offer a piece of advice or ask a question of the vampire to be ordained. Most Sabbat members will not acknowledge a leader of Archbishop rank or higher until she is ordained with a formal Blood Bath, as that clergy member has not received the blessing of Caine.
|description = 
The Ritemaster opens the ritual by giving a sermon or inspirational speech on the subject of the Sabbat. He then introduces the target of the Blood Bath and announces their new Rank. The Ritemaster describes the target's deeds and why she is ascending to her new rank.

Then each ritualist, starting with the Ritemaster, pantomimes injuring themselves in some way and anointing the target with their own blood. They then ask the target a question, or give a piece of advice to the target. Finally each ritualist spends 3 blood.

Regardless of the outcome of the ritual, the target gains the new title associated with their new rank.

If the ritual is successful, the target’s blood capacity temporarily increases by 10 for 24 hours.
