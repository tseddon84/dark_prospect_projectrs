# Ritual:Tucked Inside a Walnut Shell

{{Ritual
|Changeling|
|name = Tucked Inside a Walnut Shell
|requires = Changeling, Rituals 1
|components = A small object (no larger than a moderate sized sword) you wish to conceal, a nut (preferably a walnut, but any shelled nut of about that size will work in a pinch), orange ribbon, something to cover them both
|level = 1
|cost = 1 Glamour
|casttime = 2 minutes
|purpose = Hide a small item inside a shell, where it will remain until the shell is broken open.
|description =

The ritualist takes the object they wish to conceal and says to it. “It is time for you to sleep, all tucked away warm and cozy. While you sleep you will Dream that you are nothing more than a nut, hidden away by a squirrel, concealed deep and dark below the ground.”

The ritualist then takes the nut and says to it. “You will be the cradle in which this [[object]] sleeps. Keep them tucked away and safe from all harm. You will stay closed and wait for spring. When I knock upon you three times and say “insert word here” you will become the nut that bears gifts and you will open and restore that which was hidden.”

The ritualist then places the nut upon the item and covers them with a cloth or other item, in the manner of a stage magician. When the cover is removed, only the walnut remains (If you don’t know how to do this trick in real life, simply telling everyone the item has vanished OOG will suffice).

:Fill out a 3x5 card with the following information:
:Tucked Inside a Walnut Shell
:[Your Name]
:[Current Date]
:Only the person named above, or characters with Item Sight can see this or interact with this card.
:This card represents a walnut which contains another item. Destroying this card represents destroying the walnut, and reveals the item inside (remove the orange ribbon from the item when that happens).

Attach the card to the item to be “hidden” in the shell, and tie an orange ribbon to the item to hide it from other characters as per the Conceal power. It will remain concealed for as long as the card remains attached (this can span across multiple games). As long as the item remains concealed, the 3x5 card must be attached to it such that another character who comes across it can potentially read and/or destroy the card. It is not necessary to keep the actual walnut prop with the item, but it is encouraged if you can. The Ritualist (and only them) may knock three times on the walnut/ card and speak the defined password to end the ritual without destroying the walnut. 

A given ritualist may have a number of items tucked inside Walnuts equal to their Rituals background. Each use of this Ritual requires a separate Walnut and card.

}}
