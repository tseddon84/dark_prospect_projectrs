# Ritual:Rite of Renown

{{Ritual
|Werewolf|Fera|Bastet|Corax|Gurahl|Nuwisha|Ratkin|
|name=Rite of the Renown
|requires=Any Werewolf or Fera
|level=1
|cost=No cost
|casttime=3 minutes
|description=
If you have the Spirit Rapport merit, start by notifying the Renown Spirit. If you do not, perform appropriate chiminage to get the spirit's attention. Announce: "Spirit, I beg you to listen to our story and grant recognition of my [Renown Type]. If you have the Spirit Rapport merit and a Galliard (or Galliard equivalent is present) you may temporarily loan your Spirit Rapport to that character to tell the story. The person telling the story should do their best to tell an exciting story that covers enough renown-worthy activities to gain the desired Renown type. Upon completion, make it clear that your story is over and look at the Renown Spirit. The Spirit should decide whether your story was sufficient and if not should assign you an additional task to complete to gain your Renown. If you achieve the goal ask ST camp for the Renown Spirit's return. When completed receive a Renown tag to attach to a prop of your choosing.
