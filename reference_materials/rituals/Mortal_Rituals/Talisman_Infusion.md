Meditate uninterrupted for 5 minutes. At the end of this meditation period you may restore any number of charges to these Talismans as follows:

* You may charge any number of Connected Talismans. Each charge costs 1 of the resource listed on the Talisman tag.
* If two Connected Talismans use the same resource you may transfer charges between them.
* You may choose to completely discharge 1 Connected Talisman and remove its Connection to your character. If you do you may Connect to 1 unconnected Talisman that you own. When Connecting to a different Talisman if the discharged and charged Talismans use the same resource, you may transfer charges between them.

As long as the items remain within the circle and you remain where you performed your meditation you may continue to restore charges as above to each of these items repeatedly without casting this ritual again. Leaving the circle or entering combat ends this ritual.
