Place the melee weapon in front of you

Kneel and take the holy symbol to your patron in hand, and recite the words:

“Oh Mighty [Patron], I beesech you to bestow your blessing upon me and grant me your might. I humbly ask to harness a sliver of your power; grant me the gift of the sun instilled in this weapon before me. Allow me to guard your faithful and make those who go against your will tremble at our coming."

~*~ Perform ritual acts appropriate to your deity for one minute. ~*~

Pick up the weapon and recite the following:

"[Patron], I thank you for your gift and I will go forth with your might to face whatever trials may face me."

To complete the Ritual, spend 2 Essence. Receive Shared Strength: Elemental Weapons: Fire, 1 Use, No Cost, 60 seconds, this weapon only
