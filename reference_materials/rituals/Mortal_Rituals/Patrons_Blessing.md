# Ritual:Patron's Blessing

{{Ritual
|Mortal|Faith|
|name = Patron's Blessing
|level = 0
|requires = Faith Numina, A Removable Focus Tag to be placed on the item (2 in x 2 in tag)
|cost = 1 or more essence
|components = A holy symbol significant to or symbolic of your patron
|target = 
|casttime = 2 minutes + display time
|description = 
Kneel and take the holy symbol to your patron in hand, and say:

“[Patron], I beseech you to bestow you blessing upon me as I ask for your guidance and power."

Meditate over the holy symbol for 1 minute and then spend 1 or more essence.

Attach the Focus Tag onto your holy symbol and place the holy symbol in a visibly prominent place on your person and proclaim:

"[Patron], with your blessing I travel forth to bring your will to the world."

In 15 minutes x [Essence Spent], remove the Focus Tag and gain 3 x [Essence Spent], this occurs at the end of the given time period.

If the holy symbol is removed from your person, you remove it from prominent display, or if the Focus Tag is destroyed before you regain the energy, the ritual is disrupted and you must start the ritual over again.

Destroying the Focus Tag does not destroy the Holy Symbol itself.

'''''This ritual is depreciated as the functionality of Faith Idols has changed to no longer require a ritual to use. Any character who has this ritual may trade it in for another Faith Systemic Ritual or for a Unique Ritual of their design.'''''
}}
