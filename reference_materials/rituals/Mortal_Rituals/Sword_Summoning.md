# Ritual:Sword Summoning

{{Ritual
|Mortal|Hedge Magic|
|name=Sword Summoning
|requires=Hedge Magic, Rituals 1
|level=1
|cost=1 Essence 
|components=A weapon. A sheet or cloth large enough to conceal the weapon. An orange OOG ribbon.
|casttime= 2 minutes
|notes=
|description=The caster of this ritual spends 1 minute ritually cleaning or polishing the weapon and 30 seconds meditating with it. They then lay it down on a surface and tie the orange ribbon around it while covering it with the cloth.

The caster recites the following passage as they pass their hand over the covered weapon:

“Begone, implement of death, so that you may return to my hand when needed most.”

Spend 1 Essence.

Out of game, place the weapon on your person. It is now considered under the effect of the Conceal power until the ribbon is removed, or for 24 hours, whichever comes first.
}}
