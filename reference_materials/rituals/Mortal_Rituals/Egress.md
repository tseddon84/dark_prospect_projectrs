# Ritual:Egress

{{Ritual
|Mortal|Hedge Magic|
|name=Egress
|requires=Hedge Magic, Rituals 3
|level=3
|cost=1 Willpower
|components=Chalk, powder, or other marking implement
|casttime= 2 minutes
|description=Make a circle on the ground with your marking implement.

Draw a compass rose within the circle. The facings do not need to point in the actual directions (but it would be nice).

Starting at the eastern point, pace the circle counter-clockwise and say each line at its corresponding point:

“I call to the rising sun. Allow me to tread along the shortest of paths.<br>
I call to the warm South. Allow me to tread along the shortest of paths.<br>
I call to the setting sun. Allow me to tread along the shortest of paths.<br>
I call to the cold North. Allow me to tread along the shortest of paths."<br>

Step into the center of the circle and say the following:

“At my dire call, usher me hence.”

Spend 1 Willpower

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
<pre class="card">Ritual: Egress
Ritualist: (Your name)
Begin Time: (Current time and date)
Duration: 24 hours
Effect: Teleport [ritual location], then destroy this card.</pre>

Leave the card in the location of the ritual. Once at any time during the duration, you may Teleport to the card. The ritual effect immediately ends after using this power.
