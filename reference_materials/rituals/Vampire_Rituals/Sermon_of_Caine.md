# Ritual:Sermon of Caine

|purpose = Granting the clarity of purpose required to resist the call of the Beast and persist in the fight with the Antideluvians.
|description = To start the ritual, The Ritemaster spends a willpower and each participant spends 1 blood.

This ritual can take several forms, but the motions are all basically the same:

The ritualist gathers all of the participants together and leads a group activity regarding the concept of Caine, the origin of Vampirism or the History of the Sabbat. This can be readings from the Book of Nod, debate about the origin of Kindred, a tale of the Anarch Revolt, or simply stories of the legends of the creation of each of the clans. This discussion or reading should take no less than 15 minutes and should be formal. Casual conversation does not qualify, though the Ritemaster may invite participation from the congregation at their option.

The sermon must last at least fifteen minutes, though it may be longer. At the end of the sermon recite the following: “By the Dark Father’s damnation are we blessed.”

If successful, all participants in the ritual (congregants and Ritemaster alike) are granted the following benefit: the next time they would lose Path while pursuing the goals of the Sabbat as a whole or their Pack specifically, or following the instincts and nature of a Vampire (feeding, seeking safety from the sun or fire, etc), they may ignore that Path loss. This effect lasts until the end of the game. This works on Vampires of any Path, including Humanity (and is quite important for keeping younger members of the Sect out of Wassail while they choose their Path).

Success or failure, a Ritemaster can only perform this ritual once per day, and a given Cainite can benefit from the ritual only once per event.
