# Ritual:Ward vs. Spirits

{{Ritual
|Thaumaturgy|Vampire|Ghoul|
|name = Ward vs. Spirits
|requires = Thaumaturgy, Rituals 5
|components = A piece of parchment, a two coins, a weapon used to kill something, something to write with.
|level = 5
|cost = 5 blood
|casttime = 8 minutes
|description = Create a level 5 Thaumaturgy Circle. 
Sit in the center of the circle. Arrange the components around the circle, with one on each point.

On the parchment, draw large sigil that you feel is repellent to spirits. It should cover most of the parchment. 

At the bottom of the parchment write the following: “OOG Ward vs Spirits. If this parchment is removed from the door, remove the corresponding 3x5 on the outside of the door”

After the drawing is complete, repeat the following 5 times:<br>
Go over the same lines with the Weapon Focus, chanting the following:<br>
“By my will, this passage is proof from the Spirits.”<br>
Silently meditate on the parchment for 60 seconds

Spend 5 blood. Destroy the tag on the wooden focus.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
 This Door is ritually Warded vs those in the Umbra.
 Ritual: Ward vs. Spirits
 Caster: (Your name)
 Begin Time: (Current time and date)
 Duration: 4 hours
 Effect: This door is Warded against anyone in the Umbra, and they may not pass through this door. This ward will end if the Parchment on the other side of the door is removed. The caster is immune to this ward.

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual; it should either be done at the time of casting, or the caster must silently meditate for 1 minute if a preprinted card is used.

Affix the parchment to the inside of the door, and the 3x5 to the opposite side.

The ritual will end if the parchment is removed from the inside of the door by anyone.

Any given door can only have one Ward Vs ritual cast on it at a time.
