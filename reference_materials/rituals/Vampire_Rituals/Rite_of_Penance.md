# Ritual:Rite of Penance

{{Ritual
|Sabbat|Vampire|
|name = Rite of Penance
|level = 2
|requires = Ritemaster of the Sabbat, Rituals 2
|cost = 2 blood from each priest and the Ritemaster
|components = The Penitent, The Aggrieved, the Pack Priest of both parties, the Ritemaster
|casttime = 10 minutes or so
|purpose = The newest of the Auctoritas Ritae, this Rite allows a humiliated or failed sect member to atone for her actions. An insincere Rite of Penance may succeed, but if discovered, such a vampire will almost certainly not be given a second chance to atone. Performing a Rite of Penance is a semi-voluntary act. Evidence of wrongdoing is brought to a superior (typically either a member of the clergy or the individual’s Priest). If the evidence is compelling, the penitent vampire can request the rite in order to clear her name, or she may be ordered to perform it. If a vampire is ordered to perform a Rite of Penance by an Authority, her only options are to agree and perform the rite or to challenge her accuser to Monomacy.
|description = The penitent gathers in a public location along with her Priest, the Aggrieved, and the Aggrieved’s Priest. The Ritemaster may be either of the Priests, or a third party if neither of them know the Rite. If the individual’s actions have harmed the sect rather than an individual, the ranking member of the clergy in attendance stands as aggrieved on behalf of the sect (and also serves as the Priest of the Aggrieved). The penitent must state the deed for which she intends to make amends, though she is under no obligation to admit fault or apologize. Thereafter, the penitent’s Priest will assign an appropriate task, to be fulfilled within two nights (and the task must be reasonably possible within that time). The aggrieved may then add a caveat to this task, usually magnifying the difficulty based on whether or not she feels the penitent is truly remorseful. This caveat cannot be an entirely new task, but can be something like “…and you must do so using only a pistol,” or “…which must be retrieved from the local Prince of the Camarilla.”

After the task of contrition has been established, the penitent can choose a willing second (typically a member of her pack) to aid her in the task. That second is bound by the same caveats of the task, and the penitent can have no other assistance. 

Each Priest, and the Ritemaster, spends 2 blood. 

If the penitent does not succeed in the task within two nights, or if she accepts assistance from any other member of the Sabbat, the ritual fails, and both Priests become aware of the failure. They should be informed OOC as soon as possible.

If the ritual succeeds, the Penitent is considered forgiven for the actions described. If they fail, they must immediately step down from any Sabbat title or position and may not hold any position for 6 months.
