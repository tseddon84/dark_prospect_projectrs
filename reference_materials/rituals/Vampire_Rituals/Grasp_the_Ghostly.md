# Ritual:Grasp the Ghostly

{{Ritual
|Vampire|Necromancy|Ghoul|
|name = Grasp the Ghostly
|level = 5
|requires = Necromancy, Rituals 5
|cost = 1 blood or 10 blood
|components = Black bandages, a large wooden basin or bowl. Two target items
|purpose = 
|casttime = 6 minutes or 16 minutes
|notes = This ritual has two uses
|description = Prepare your necromantic foci

Ritually wrap both of your hands in black bandages while chanting a prayer for the dead.

Spend 1 blood.

The ritualist gains Gauntlet Grasp, No Cost, 1 Hour. The ritualist can stop there, or keep going for the second effect.

The second effect allows the Necromancer to transfer the Relic tag from any object to any other object of roughly equal size and weight.

Pantomime biting your wrist through the bandages and bleeding into the basin. Ritually wash the relic item you wish to transfer the tag from in the blood from the basin. Then ritually wash the item you wish to transfer the tag to. This process should take at least 5 minutes.

Spend 9 blood. (With the first part of the ritual, that’s 10 blood total.)

Transfer the relic tag from the one object to the other. This transfer is permanent, unless transferred back with another usage of this ritual.}}
