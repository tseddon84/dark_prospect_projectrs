Prepare a contract with the target where they commit themselves to the necromancer, even beyond death. Have the target sign the contract in red ink representing blood. (The target should take 1 damage or spend 1 blood.)

Spend 2 blood.

On a white 3x5 card, write the following:
<pre class="card">
Ritual: The Servant Undying
Caster: (Your name)
Target: (Target’s Name)
Begin Time: (Current time and date)
Duration: Until End of Game
Effect: When the Target enters the Dying state, they immediately have Zombie, No Cost, 10 minutes cast on them. While in this state, they are commanded to: (Preset command)
</pre>

Writing the 3x5 card is part of casting the ritual; it should either be done at the time of casting, or the caster must silently meditate for 1 minute if a preprinted card is used.

The target should carry the ritual card with them.
