# Ritual:Rite of the Firedance

{{Ritual
|Sabbat|Vampire|
|name = Rite of the Firedance
|level = 3
|requires = Ritemaster of the Sabbat, Rituals 3
|cost = 1-15 points of aggravated damage which may be resisted or dodged (see text)
|components = A prop or space to represent a large bonfire (which should not be on fire out of game), at least 3 Vampires
|casttime = 5 minutes or more
|purpose = Demonstrate the participant's courage and contempt for the banes of Vampire kind, instilling them with the power to resist fear and frenzy.
|description = Set out either a prop which can credibly represent a large bonfire, or else define a circle of at least three feet in diameter to represent the borders of the flame (possibly with a small fire prop in the middle). The ritual participants should stand around the flame, facing inward, while the ritemaster stokes the fire for a minute. The other participants are encouraged to hoot and/or holler while the flames are stoked, bragging about their prowess and courage. Once a minute has passed, the Ritemaster should step in front of the other participants and gesture for silence (it is traditional to ignore the Ritemaster here the first few times, make them work for it). Once the Ritemaster has the assembled vampire’s attention, he should ask “Who will brave the flames?”

Each participant should step forward in turn (jockeying for who gets to go first is encouraged), and mime jumping over the bonfire or dancing through the flames (crossing from one end of the circle to the other). Doing so causes the participant 1 point of aggravated damage, which may be Resisted or Dodged as though originating from a physical attack. If any participant refuses to Brave the Flames this first time, the ritual fails (damage already dealt to other participants is not healed).

Once everyone has Braved the Flames, the Ritemaster should once again ask “Who will Brave the Flames?” Any participant willing to do so may once again step forward, though it is no longer mandatory for all participants to continue to Brave the Flames at this point. The Ritemaster, however, must always Brave the Flames if any other character in the Rite is willing to, or the Rite fails.

If a character falls unconscious during the Rite, the Rite is not interrupted unless the other participants stop to help them. If the Ritemaster is incapacitated, the Rite continues as long as another participant takes their role.

The Flames may be Braved up to 5 times by each participant. Each time a character Braves the Flames the aggravated damage dealt increases by 1. The Vampire may not Recover during the Rite, nor may they spend Willpower to gain the effects of a Recovery instantly.

Once no one remains who is willing to Brave the Flames (or the Flames have been Braved a total of 5 times), the ritemaster reads the following: “For each time you Braved the Flames, gain 1 free Resist against either the Terror power, or any form of Frenzy. These Resists last until the next sunrise.”
