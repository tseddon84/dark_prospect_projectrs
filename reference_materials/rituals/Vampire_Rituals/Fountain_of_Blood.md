 This Node is attackable and has 10 Health.
 If this Node reaches 0 health, remove this card and restore the original Node Card.

Fill the baggie with 10 tokens. The effect on the card becomes immediately active, providing 1 Blood to all participants immediately and every 1 minute thereafter.
