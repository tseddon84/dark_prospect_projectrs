# Ritual:Rite of Summoning

{{Ritual
|Werewolf|Fera|Bastet|Corax|Gurahl|Nuwisha|Ratkin|
|name=Rite of Summoning
|requires=1 Shifter of any sort - Spirit Rapport may increase chance of success
|level=2
|cost=No Cost
|renown=Wisdom
|casttime=5 minutes
|purpose=Allows a shifter to request a specific spirit or spirit type from ST camp during game. ST camp is not required to respond to the summons.
|description=
The ritualist must sit on the ground facing a light source - it need not be a fire, a lantern is sufficient. Chant for 5 minutes, then place on an orange headband and head to ST camp. Leave a 3x5 card with the Storytellers with the following text:

<pre>
Ritual of Summoning
[Your name], [Your character name]
[Spirit / Type of spirit you wish to summon]
[Time of summoning]
[Whether or not you have Spirit Rapport]
</pre>

The storytellers may decide whether to send the spirit or not, but they will accept the request. You may request spirits whenever you wish during Downtime, but during uptime you should not request specific spirits without this rite.
}}
