# Ritual:Petition The Spirit's Boon

This ritual must be cast in the umbra.

Draw a ritual circle on the ground. Both the ritualist and a Gaian Spirit with the obedience power step into the circle. The ritualist and the spirit negotiate chiminage. 

When the chiminage is agreed upon the ritualist spends the energy and says.
"By Gaia's Will"
The Ritualist and any Shifters touching the circle may immediately grant Gnosis to the spirit. This Gnosis may take the spirit above its maximum pool.

For the next 60 seconds, or until they leave the circle, the spirit gains shared strength for any one of the following powers that it has. Any power with a duration greater than 1 hour is only shared at a 1 hour duration.
* Wyld - Chaos: Breach, 1 Gnosis, Self
* Wyld - Creation: Trash is Treasure, 1 Gnosis, Self, 10 minutes
* Weaver - Control: Honesty, 1 Gnosis, Other, 1 question
* Weaver - Stasis: Ward Door: Any, 1 Gnosis, Doorway
* Wyrm - Corruption: Apply Taint: Wyrm, 1-5 Gnosis, Other
* Wyrm - Destruction: Enrage, 1 Gnosis, Other
* Wyrm - Devouring: Cloak, No Cost, Self
* General - Bypass 1, No Cost, Self
* Aggressor - Elemental: Element 1, 1 Gnosis, Other (as the spirit's appropriate level in that tree)
* Aggressor - Physical: Augment, 1 Gnosis, Self
* Defender - Escape: Hasty Escape, 1 Gnosis, Self, 10 minutes
* Defender - Protection: Interference, 1 Gnosis, Self
* Regenerator - Regrowth: Remove Agg, 1 Gnosis, Self
* Regenerator - Reversion: Cleanse Taint, 1 Gnosis, Self
* Restorer - Renewal: Healing Touch 2, 1 Gnosis, Other (as the spirit's appropriate level in that tree)
* Restorer - Purification: Cleanse Taint, 1 Gnosis, Other
* Perceiver - Sight: Cloak Sight, No Cost, Self, Always On
* Perceiver - Sense: Sense Taint: Any, 1 Gnosis, Other
* Manipulator - Ride: Obedience, 1 Gnosis, Other, 10 minutes
* Manipulator - Effect: Confusion, 1 Gnosis, Other, 10 minutes

Any excess Gnosis above the spirits maximum pool is lost when this effect ends.
