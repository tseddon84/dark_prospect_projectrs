# Ritual:The Pact of Doom

{{Ritual
|Wraith|
|name = The Pact of Doom
|requires = Wraith, Castigate, Rituals 3
|components = The ritualist and one or more Wraiths (as well as their Shadows)
|level = 3
|cost = Each participant gains Angst equal to their Shadow Rating
|casttime = 5 minutes (Or so)
|purpose = Grant a Wraith's Psyche the ability to utilize their Shadow's Dark Arcanoi.
|description = 
-The ritualist gathers the participants before themselves

-Ask each participant why they are invoking the pact, and explain the risks to mind, memory and soul.

-After each explanation has been provided, each participant swears that they understand and accept the risks, that they will not falter in the face of oblivion, and that they will use the pact's power in defense of Creation.

-The ritualist may make a special "Mass Shadowspeech: Do you consent to the pact?" call - Only wraiths whose shadows answer in the affirmative may benefit from this rite. If a shadow declines, that wraith is no longer a participant in the ritual.

-The ritualist then declares "It has been witnessed, the pact is sworn and sealed. May all present slay their doom."

Upon completion of the ritual, each Wraith participant who's Shadow agreed to the pact then gains Angst equal to their Shadow Rating. For the remainder of the event, each such wraith my use any Dark Arcanoi their shadow knows in exchange for gaining Angst equal to the power's normal Angst Cost. A Participant uses their Dark Arcanoi normally while in Catharsis.

Dark Arcanoi a psyche gains access to through this ritual may never be taught, stolen, borrowed, or provided via Shared Strength or similar effects. If another character attempts to use Thieving Talons, Borrow Power, or a similar effect to gain access to a dark arcanoi power granted by the ritual the reply is "Immune". Maleficence 3 may not be used to grant Dark Arcanoi in this way (unless used while the Shadow is in control, as normal), but may be used to give other Arcanoi the Wraith knows.

Warning: a wraith's Shadow provides this power by tapping into the hive mind of Oblivion to provide the knowledge to the psyche. Being connected to the spectral hivemind may carry unexpected consequences, at ST discretion.

}}
