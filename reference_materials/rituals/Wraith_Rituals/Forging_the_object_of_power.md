Level: 4

Passage: "Malleo loquar tibi ignem et nunc et semper, et in potentia fasinum."

This is where turning a Soul into a Thing gets really interesting.

The economy of the soul trade, is that one item, is one soul. It matters not how big or small the item is, every one of them requires the forging of a plasmic or human soul.

This ritual allows you to turn an Oboli into a Fetish Item or even to turn a Wraith or Plasmic character directly into a such an item

Forge a Wraith or Plasmic character into an Obolis by placing them in a Soulforge, reciting the required phrase (the target does not suffer exposure damage from using this power). Place the target on the ground and spend 60 seconds roleplaying forging the target into the desired item then spend the required Pathos.

The Pathos requirement is equal to the level of Talisman or Fetish that you create. Pathos spent in this Ritual decreases your maximum Pathos for the remainder of game. If you are directly forging a character, gain 4 Angst.
