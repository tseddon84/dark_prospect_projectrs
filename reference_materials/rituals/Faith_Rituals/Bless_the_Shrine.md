# Ritual:Bless the Shrine

{{Ritual
|Mortal|Faith|
|name = Bless the Shrine
|level = 2
|requires = Faith Numina, Rituals 2
|cost = 1 Willpower
|components = Materials to construct/maintain a shrine to your patron
|target = 
|casttime = 3 minutes
|purpose = Creates an essence energy node for followers of a specific patron
|description = 
Clean the area around your existing shrine or where you intend to construct a new shrine. Build a shrine appropriate your patron or ensure that your existing shrine is properly maintained and does not appear vandalized.

Pace the area around your shrine, spending 1 minute paying homage to your patron in a way that is appropriate to them, and then recite the following:

[Patron], [I/We] wish for your blessing. Empower your faithful so that they may enact your will.

On a white 3x5 card write the following and place it next to the shrine:
 Ritual: Bless the Shrine
 Ritualist: (Your name)
 Begin Time: (Current time and date)
 Duration: 1 Hour
 While within 10 feet of this card gain 1 essence every 5 minutes if your patron is [Ritualist’s Patron]
}}
