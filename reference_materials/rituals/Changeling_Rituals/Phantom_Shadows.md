“From the Dreaming, I summon you.
Chimera, be conjured!”

The Dreaming is fickle and doesn’t always respond in linear time. (IE, there has to be a spare NPC available for this to work.) The ritualist is presented with a blank slate chimera (NPC in neutral garb) that they prepare by giving them costuming, props and make-up of their own. The ritualist is responsible for dressing up the NPC, and in the process briefing them on their identity and function, conjuring the chimera from raw dream-stuff. 

The Chimera will only last an hour. It has 10 health levels, no will power, and no powers other than Gauntlet Perception. It has the Brawl Ability, and may gain one of Melee, Firearms or Archery if they're appropriate to its role (a knight for melee, a huntsman for archery, or a dastardly bandit for firearms). The Chimera is Umbra-Bound under normal circumstances, though it can be Shunted normally. It takes 1 aggravated damage for every minute it spends in the Realm (as Exposure damage) unless touching a Changeling or in the radius of a Glamour node, and any time it hears someone verbally deny the existence of magic, it takes 1 damage. It will play the role it was designed to do to the best of its ability. 

A Chimera may have almost any appearance that the NPC can rep, as if affected by the Visage power. It may visually imitate the appearance of a mask or mantle power, without any of the benefits or abilities associated with them. A chimerical werewolf or other animal mask still triggers delirium should they be seen by a normal human, but the summoned chimera will generally also be in an unpleasant way.
