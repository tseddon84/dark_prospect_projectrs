# Ritual:Will o the Wisp

{{Ritual
|Changeling|
|name = Will o' The Wisp
|requires = Changeling, Soothsay, Rituals 2
|components = Paper, art supplies, supplies to pretend to make a fire, an ST capable of guiding the ritual, a prop representing a flame
|level = 2
|cost = 3 Glamour
|casttime = 5 or more minutes

|purpose = Locate an object, person or place with ST assistance
|description = This ritual requires the presence of an ST to work. A player intending to use it may go to ST camp to request an ST be made available. 

Draw an image on paper of what you are searching for. Imbue the image with 3 glamour as you draw it, then  state “I know what I seek.” then say what it is you seek.

Once the drawing is complete, pantomime setting it aflame. As you do so, state, “Will O’ The Wisp, guide us with your light.  May your flame light the way to journey’s end.”

The ST will then provide a fire prop representing a Will o’ The Wisp, , and with it, lead the caster towards their goal, but not necessarily in the most direct, safe, or logical way.  If the caster’s goal is unavailable, the Will o’ the Wisp may instead lead them to something else at ST discretion.
