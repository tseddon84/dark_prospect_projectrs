'''Target''': One Changeling who does not yet know their True Name.

The ritualist writes runes in a circle around the Changeling. Then, they grasp the hands of the target Changeling who has not yet undergone this ritual before. 

“Let us Sain this Kithain, reborn into the Autumn World! 
May we pierce the Mists that keep them from their Truth.

Within your Faerie Soul is a True Name.
It is hidden to you now, as so much has been forgotten.
Yet, through Rune and Spell, shall you remember.

Through Autumn’s Growing Shadows, I call thy True Name. 
Through Winter’s Cold Chill, I call thy True Name. 
Through Spring’s Rites of Rebirth, I call thy True Name. 
Through Summer’s Blazing Heat, I call thy True Name

Knowledge of thy True Name grants power unto you.
Respect it. 
Knowledge of thy True Name grants others power over you.
Keep it Secret.

As it has been since the Shattering, may you now understand the faerie soul that dwells within that mortal body. 

Go now, a child of the Dreaming, blessed with the knowledge of who you are and have always been.” (The ritualist spends 1 glamour and 1 willpower.)

The Ritualist dramatically ends the ritual, with the stamping of a staff, the clapping of hands, drumming, or a laying on of hands. The Changeling target now realizes the full truth of their Faerie existence, including their full True Name, and is formally a part of Changeling society– what, exactly, their True Name and Nature is will be determined by the Changeling’s player.
