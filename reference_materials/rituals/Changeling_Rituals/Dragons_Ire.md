# Ritual:Dragon's Ire

{{Ritual
|Changeling|
|name = Dragon's Ire
|requires = Changeling, Metamorphosis, Rituals 4
|components = Facepaint or makeup
|level = 4
|cost = 4 Glamour
|casttime = 10 minutes
|purpose = Grant martial prowess to a character seeking revenge
|description = The ritualist paints the face of the target with bold geometric lines. As they paint the face of the target, they recite the following:

“To trip the ire of a dragon is foolish.
Their might, legendary.
Their power, terrifying
Let a dragon’s power flow within thy veins.
May your blows strike with a dragon’s might
Fueled by a dragon’s Ire.
Tell me warrior, who has gained thy Ire, thy righteous anger?

The target of the ritualist must answer the question, truthfully. If they lie, the ritual fails (the ritualist should make this clear to the player out of game).

“Then, until the sun next rises, shall the might and power of a dragon flow through you
So that you may fight as a dragon would.”

The ritualist spends 4 glamour.

Until the next sunrise, the target of the ritual can only attack the named victim. They may defend themselves from others, but may not attack another without causing the ritual to immediately end.

The target gains the following powers for the duration:
*Imprint, always on, self
*Augment 1, 1 Glamour, self, 60 seconds
*Energy to Health, 1 Glamour per Health, once per recovery, self. 


}}
