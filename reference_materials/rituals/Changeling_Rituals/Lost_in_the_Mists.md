# Ritual:Lost in the Mists

{{Ritual
|Changeling|
|name = Lost in the Mists
|requires = Changeling, Chicainery or Delusion, Rituals 3
|components = A blanked and a cup of water
|level = 3
|cost = 3
|casttime = 5 minutes
|purpose = Cloud the memories of a target, giving them temporary amnesia
|description = The ritualist acts out winds that surround the sleeping target, sprinkling water around the target. 

“The Mists come.
Your Memory Fades.
Who are you?

“You forget who you are.
You forget who you were.
Who are you?”

The ritualist then places a blanket over the target. 

“For the next hour, you don’t remember your identity. 
You will be confused, and easily misled.
Awaken now, bereft of identity. 
Amnesia falls upon you. 
Exit the mists, and ask, Who are You?”

The ritualist removes the blanket. 

The target will awaken with amnesia and be easily susceptible to suggestions about who they actually are. This will last for an hour, or until they take more than 1 level of damage. For the duration of the effect, the target is also Enchanted, and will Rationalize away any events that occurred during their amnesia as happening in a dream.


}}
