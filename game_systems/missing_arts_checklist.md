# Missing Arts Checklist

This document tracks the canonical *Changeling: The Dreaming* Arts that have not yet been adapted into the *Dark Prospects* LARP ruleset. 

As we draft a LARP-safe, 5-level system for each Art, we will check them off this list.

## The Core Arts
- [ ] **Dragon's Ire** - The Art of combat, fury, and battle prowess.
- [ ] **Pyretics** - The Art of fire, intense emotion, and heat manipulation.
- [ ] **Contract** - The Art of oaths, bindings, and metaphysical deals.
- [ ] **Infusion** - The Art of crafting, enhancing, and animating objects.

## The Seasonal Arts (C20)

- [ ] **Summer** - The Art of vigor, blinding sunlight, and wrath.
